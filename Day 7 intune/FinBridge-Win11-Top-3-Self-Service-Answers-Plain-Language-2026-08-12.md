## 1) Cannot sign in or account is locked

Please try this once: sign out fully, wait 10 minutes, then sign in again. If it still fails, restart your device and try one more time from the normal sign-in screen. Do not keep retrying if it still does not work.

Contact the service desk if you are still locked out. Share your username, when it started, the exact message on screen, and how many sign-in attempts you made.

## 2) OneDrive files are missing

Open OneDrive and check if it is running. Wait 3-5 minutes, then refresh the folder. If files still do not appear, restart your device once and check again. You can also check the OneDrive Recycle Bin.

Contact the service desk if files are still missing. Share the file name, where you expected to see it, when you last saw it, and whether it is missing on web, desktop, or both.

## 3) Shared drive says access denied

Close the shared drive window and try again after a few minutes. If you use VPN, make sure it is connected before trying again. If you still see access denied, stop retrying.

Contact the service desk if access still fails. Share the drive name, the exact message shown, when it started, and what work is blocked.
