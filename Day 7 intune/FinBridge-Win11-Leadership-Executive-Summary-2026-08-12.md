FinBridge’s Windows 11 migration is now trending in the right direction for most employees. The biggest week 1 pain points, especially sign-in lockouts, missing cloud files, and unstable remote access, have largely settled, and user feedback has shifted from disruption toward smoother day-to-day working. Overall confidence is improving, with more staff reporting that core tasks are working as expected and that reliability has improved compared with the first week.

Two areas still need focused attention. First, the Floor 3 printing issue remains unresolved and is now affecting team confidence because it has lasted across multiple weeks; this is being escalated with a priority recovery plan and clearer user updates until service is stable. Second, a new issue has emerged where large spreadsheet work is crashing for some users, which is now a business productivity risk for finance workflows; this is being actively triaged with targeted fixes and interim guidance to reduce disruption. In parallel, the finance laptop startup slowdown detected after a recent policy rollout is under active review, with controlled checks and staged adjustments underway to improve morning readiness while keeping security requirements in place.
