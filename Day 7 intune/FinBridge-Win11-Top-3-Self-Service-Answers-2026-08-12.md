## 1) AVD sign-in or account lockout

Try signing out fully and then signing back in once after 10 minutes. If that does not work, restart your device and try again from the normal sign-in screen. If you still cannot get in, stop trying so the lock does not get worse.

Connect the service desk right away if you are still locked out or cannot reach AVD. Share your username, the time it started, any error message you saw, and how many times you tried to sign in.

## 2) OneDrive files missing or not showing

Open OneDrive and check whether it is still running. Then wait a few minutes and refresh the folder or restart your device once. If the files still do not appear, check the Recycle Bin in OneDrive or on your device.

Connect the service desk if your files are still missing. Share the file name, where you expected to find it, when you last saw it, and whether it is missing on the web, on your desktop, or both.

## 3) Shared drive access denied

Try closing your files and opening the shared drive again after a few minutes. If you use a VPN, make sure it is connected before you try again. If you still see access denied, do not keep retrying.

Connect the service desk if the shared drive still will not open. Share the drive name, the exact message you see, the time it happened, and what work you were trying to finish.
