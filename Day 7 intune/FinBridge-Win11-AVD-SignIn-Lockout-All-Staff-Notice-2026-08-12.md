**Important update: AVD sign-in and lockout issues**

We know some users are currently having trouble signing in or are getting locked out, and the team is actively working on it.

In the meantime, please try this once: fully sign out, wait 10 minutes, and sign back in again. If that does not help, restart your device and try from the normal sign-in screen. If you are still locked out, stop retrying so it does not get worse.

We will share the next update later today as soon as we have more to report.

Please stay calm and avoid sending repeat tickets unless you are still unable to get in. If you do need the service desk, share your username, the time the issue started, any error message you saw, and how many times you tried to sign in.
