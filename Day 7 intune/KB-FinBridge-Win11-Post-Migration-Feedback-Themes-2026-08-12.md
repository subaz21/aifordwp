# KB: Fin Bridge Win11 Post-Migration Feedback Themes

- Date: 2026-08-12
- Scope: 50 end-user comments from Fin Bridge staff after Windows 11 migration
- Purpose: Cluster feedback into actionable themes for operations and support

## Severity Definitions
- Blocker: User cannot work at all
- Friction: User can work, but productivity is significantly reduced
- Minor: Cosmetic or low-impact issue
- Positive: No issue reported

## Theme Clusters

### 1) AVD Sign-In and Account Lockouts
- Count: 7
- Severity: Blocker
- Representative quotes:
  - "Cannot log in to AVD at all since this morning. Tried 3 times. Urgent."
  - "Cannot sign in to AVD, keeps saying account locked."

### 2) Floor 3 Printer Mapping Failures
- Count: 6
- Severity: Friction
- Representative quotes:
  - "Printer on floor 3 still broken, whole team can't print client docs."
  - "Printer on floor 3 -- team has given up and is walking to floor 2 to print."

### 3) Shared Drive Access Denied (S: and Finance Share)
- Count: 3
- Severity: Blocker
- Representative quotes:
  - "Can't access shared drive S: -- says permission denied."
  - "Cannot access S drive, blocking me from finishing month-end reports."

### 4) OneDrive Missing Files and Sync Errors
- Count: 4
- Severity: Blocker
- Representative quotes:
  - "My OneDrive files are missing! This is urgent, need them for a meeting."
  - "OneDrive says files missing, I need the Q1 report by end of day."

### 5) VPN Instability and Frequent Disconnects
- Count: 4
- Severity: Friction
- Representative quotes:
  - "VPN keeps dropping every 10 minutes, very frustrating for calls."
  - "VPN dropped 4 times in one hour today, unacceptable for client work."

### 6) Slow Login Performance

- Severity: Friction

### 7) Desktop/Profile Reset and Missing Shortcuts or Files

  - "Files that used to be on my desktop are just gone now."

### 8) UI and App Discoverability Changes

### 9) Positive Migration Experience and Performance Gains
- Count: 9
- Severity: Positive
- Representative quotes:
  - "Love how fast the new laptop is! Much better than before."
  - "No issues at all on my end, migration was seamless."
