# Fin Bridge Win11 Post-Migration Feedback

Date: 2026-08-12
Source: 50 end-user comments
Method: Prioritization weighted by impact first (Blocker > Friction > Minor), then volume within the same impact band.

## Top 3 Themes to Act on Today

### 1) AVD Sign-In and Account Lockouts (Count: 7, Severity: Blocker)
Why it ranks here:
- Highest-impact issue type because users cannot access their workspace at all.
- Highest volume among all Blocker themes, indicating broad operational disruption.
- Affects first-task-of-day access and creates immediate business interruption.

One sentence for manager:
- AVD sign-in and lockout failures are the top priority today because they fully block work for the largest number of impacted users.

### 2) OneDrive Missing Files and Sync Errors (Count: 4, Severity: Blocker)
Why it ranks here:
- Direct data availability risk with users reporting missing files needed for time-sensitive deliverables.
- Still a Blocker class issue because users cannot complete required tasks without files.
- Lower volume than AVD lockouts, but impact per incident remains critical.

One sentence for manager:
- OneDrive missing-file incidents are second priority because they stop users from accessing business-critical documents and create immediate delivery risk.

### 3) Shared Drive Access Denied (S: and Finance Share) (Count: 3, Severity: Blocker)
Why it ranks here:
- Permission-denied access to core shared drives blocks key finance workflows such as month-end reporting.
- Blocker severity places it ahead of higher-volume Friction themes.
- Lower count than the first two Blockers, so it ranks third.

One sentence for manager:
- Shared drive access denial is third priority because it fully blocks finance output despite affecting fewer users than the top two Blockers.

## Why High-Count Friction Themes Are Not in Top 3 Today
- Floor 3 printer mapping failures (Count: 6, Friction) and UI/discoverability changes (Count: 9, Minor) affect more users, but they do not completely stop work.
- Under impact-first prioritization, Blocker themes outrank Friction and Minor even at lower volume.
