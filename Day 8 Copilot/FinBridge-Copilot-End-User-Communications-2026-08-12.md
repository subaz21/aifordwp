# FinBridge Copilot End-User Communications (2026-08-12)

Use these messages to respond to users in plain English.

## 1) Finance lead: Cannot summarise Q3 board pack in SharePoint
Thanks for reporting this. You can open the board pack, but Copilot may not use it straight away if the file is still being indexed or has extra protection settings. This does not mean your access is broken. Please wait 30-60 minutes, then try again. If it still fails, send us the file link and time of your last attempt so we can check the file settings and indexing status.

## 2) New hire: Outlook Copilot does not know recent emails
Thanks for flagging this. As you only started yesterday, your mailbox and Copilot profile may still be completing setup in the background. This is common for new starters. Please sign out and back in to Outlook, then try again later today. If there is no change by the next working day, contact Service Desk and we will check your license and account setup.

## 3) HR manager: Word Copilot says it does not have access to salary spreadsheet
This message usually means the file is protected by sensitive access controls. That is expected for salary data and helps keep information secure. Please confirm whether you need access for this task. If yes, raise an access request through the normal HR approval route and include the spreadsheet link. Once approved, we will recheck Copilot access.

## 4) Sales rep: Teams Copilot cannot find client contract shared by guest link
Thanks for reporting this. Contracts shared from another company by guest link may not be available for Copilot search and summary in the same way as internal files. Please open the link directly to confirm you can still view the document. If you need Copilot support on this file, ask the file owner to provide an approved internal copy in our tenant, then try again.

## 5) Finance team: Copilot stopped working this morning
We are aware that Copilot access changed for multiple Finance users this morning and we are investigating as priority. This is most likely a setup or policy issue, not anything you did. Please restart your Office app and sign in again once. If still unavailable, no further action is needed from you right now. IT will post an update once service is restored.

## 6) Manager: Copilot summarized a file from a folder you forgot about
Thanks for raising this. Copilot can only use files your account can already access. This suggests you still had valid access to that folder. Please review whether you still need that access. If not, ask Service Desk to remove it. Reporting this was the right step and helps us tighten access controls.

## 7) Analyst: Copilot gives generic answers and not internal SharePoint content
Thanks for reporting this. Generic responses usually happen when Copilot cannot use your internal content yet, often due to setup, sign-in, or content readiness. Please close and reopen Office/Teams, confirm you are signed in with your work account, and try a prompt that names a specific internal file. If results are still generic, contact Service Desk and include one example prompt.

## 8) Executive assistant: Outlook Copilot cannot see shared mailbox calendar
Thanks for reporting this. Shared or delegated mailbox calendars can behave differently from your own mailbox in Copilot. First, confirm you can open the shared calendar normally in Outlook. If yes but Copilot still cannot use it, send Service Desk the mailbox name and your delegate level so we can verify supported access and permissions.

## Standard Closing Line (optional)
If you need help with any of the steps above, contact Service Desk and include screenshots, exact error text, and the time of your last test.
