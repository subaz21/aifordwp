# Help Guide: Black Screen When Logging In

Version: v 1.0  
Date: 07/08/2026  
Status: Draft

If your work desktop shows a black screen when you sign in, don’t worry. Your files and data are safe.

## What you can do now

1. Wait 60 seconds on the black screen.
2. If nothing changes, close the session and sign in again one time.
3. If the screen is still black, sign out fully and sign in again.
4. If it still continues, restart your computer and try again.
5. Make sure your internet connection is stable before you try again.

## What not to do

- Do not try many logins in a row very quickly.
- Do not change computer settings.

## If you need help

Contact the IT Help Team if the black screen happens again after the steps above, if your session keeps closing, or if you cannot get to your desktop within a few minutes.

## Details to include in your help request

Please share these details so support can help faster:

- Your full name and work login name
- The time the issue started (include your local time)
- Your computer name (if known)
- Your location (office/home) and internet type (office Wi-Fi/home Wi-Fi/phone hotspot)
- What you saw on screen (black screen, session closed message, or both)
- Which step(s) you tried from this article
- Whether any step worked, even briefly
- A screen photo (or picture of the screen), if possible

Thank you for your patience. Your data remains safe, and support will help restore access as quickly as possible.
