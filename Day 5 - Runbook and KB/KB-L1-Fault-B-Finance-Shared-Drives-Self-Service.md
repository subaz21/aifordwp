# Help Guide: Finance Shared Drive Not Opening

Version: v 1.0  
Date: 07/08/2026  
Status: Draft

If your Finance shared drive is not opening, don’t worry. Your files and data are safe.

## What you can do now

1. Wait 60 seconds, then try opening the Finance shared drive again.
2. Sign out of your work desktop, then sign in again once.
3. Restart your computer, then sign in and check the Finance shared drive.
4. Make sure your internet connection is stable before trying again.
5. If the drive letter is missing, open File Explorer and check This PC again after sign-in.

## What not to do

- Do not try many quick sign-ins in a row.
- Do not change computer settings yourself.
- Do not map or remap work drives manually unless IT asks you to.

## If you need help

Contact the Service Desk if the Finance shared drive still does not open after these steps, or if it opens and closes again.

## Details to include in your help request

Please include the details below so support can fix this faster:

- Your full name and work login name
- Time the issue started (local time)
- Your computer name (if known)
- Your location (office/home) and internet type (office Wi-Fi/home Wi-Fi/phone hotspot)
- What you saw (drive missing, drive not opening, or access message)
- Which steps you tried from this guide
- Whether any step worked, even briefly
- A screen photo if possible

Thank you for your patience. Your data remains safe, and support will help restore access as quickly as possible.
